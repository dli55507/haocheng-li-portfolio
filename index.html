Light as Wind

A dreamy pixel-art side-scrolling adventure across the ocean, toward a faraway sky. Play as three plush heroes among sea creatures whose movement is fully procedurally animated.

My contributions include, but are not limited to:

  • Level design for Levels 04, 06, and 10
  • Designed interaction mechanics across levels, engineered gameplay logic and effects, and did QA and bug fixing
  • Helped shape the game’s foundation as a team: a custom render pipeline for perspective pixel art, and fully procedural animation with no skeletal rigs
  • Real-time physics and game feel: wind-blown paper, the swordfish’s swim, the crab’s vine swing
  • Shaders and destruction: a Worley-noise “air wall” and runtime mesh fracturing
